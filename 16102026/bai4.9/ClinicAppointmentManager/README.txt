CLINIC APPOINTMENT MANAGER

Yeu cau:
- Windows 10/11 va .NET 8 SDK.
- Mo file ClinicAppointmentManager.csproj bang Visual Studio 2022 tro len.
- Chon Build > Build Solution, sau do nhan F5.

Tat ca giao dien duoc tao bang code C#, khong can dung Designer.
Du lieu mau duoc nap khi khoi dong.
Du lieu chi luu trong bo nho va se mat khi dong ung dung.

Kiem thu:
1. Them so dien thoai khong hop le, vi du 123, phai hien ErrorProvider va khong luu.
2. Chon ngay gio trong qua khu, phai hien ErrorProvider va khong luu.
3. Nhap mot so dien thoai trong o tim kiem, bang chi hien lich co so dien thoai khop.
4. Bat loc ngay va/hoac trang thai, bang va StatusStrip phai cap nhat.
5. Chon mot dong, sua trang thai roi bam Cap nhat; bang phai hien trang thai moi.
6. Chon dong va bam Xoa; chi xoa sau khi xac nhan.
7. Vao menu Tep > Xuat danh sach dang hien thi ra CSV de xuat ket qua hien tai.

Luu y:
So dien thoai duoc kiem tra theo quy tac 10 chu so, bat dau bang 0.
